## What bit the last session (2026-11-22 to 24)

- **The key loop ends at gpg-agent's 24-hour cap**
  (`max-cache-ttl` 86400). Run `keys-warm` (no argument) at once after
  every unlock, and check `systemctl --user is-active keys-warm` in each
  status update. Missing that left the keys locked overnight.
- **Read `push-ci.sh`'s last line before any push above its head.** Once
  a plan commit was pushed on top of a chain whose CI had failed, and
  `main` was red for 25 minutes.
- **Rebasing one chain onto another changes the lock's outputs.** A
  chain rebased onto another session's chain needs `push-ci`, not a
  direct push: an output of one chain's lock call can change because of
  the other chain's code (78c on fixup-24).
- **Read with the Read tool or `grep`, `head`, `tail`, `cut`, `wc`.** A
  `sed`/`awk` read or a `VAR=` prefix waits on auto mode's classifier
  for the author (a whole night on 2026-11-22). The briefs say so.
- **A worker can sit idle after its background run ends** if nothing
  wakes it. If a worker is idle with "I'll continue when … finishes",
  check its processes and nudge it.
