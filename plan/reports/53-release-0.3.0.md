### Outcome

Four designers drew the t of `sequentium` as the turnstile and ranked it
first: the two the brief named, Fable 5.1 (this session) and Opus 5.5
(`linlog-mark-opus`), each given the author's idea to weigh first, and
the two fresh designers (`linlog-mark-free-fable`, `linlog-mark-free-opus`)
the author asked for during the session, started on a brief that named no
turnstile, no candidate and no earlier process, who arrived at it on their
own. Each gave the same reason: ⊢ is the sign every suite of the house
writes, as ⊸ is the linear suite's own, and the member's grammar (one
letter of the word becomes the calculus's sign, in the word's own stem
weight) is continued without copying the member. The exchange settled its
drawing: the t's stem to the t's own height (656), squared, without
Figtree's foot; the crossbar's right arm alone, drawn out to 250 units in
the t's crossbar band (−500 to −388, 112 thick); the letters after it moved
87 units so that the i's gap is the t's own; the square a turnstile with
64-unit strokes on the 32-unit grid, crisp at 16 pixels. The word is read
first and the sign second, as the lollipop's i stays an i; the drawing with
the arm at the middle of the stem, which two designers made and the
partner first preferred, is shown beside it as the alternative reading.

The shortlist for the author has three candidates: that turnstile; `⊢
sequentium`, the turnstile before the whole word, found by both anchored
designers apart, with the square `⊢s`; and `sequentium,`, the comma, the
partner's bold alternative, with the square `s,`, last and with this
session's disagreement stated. All three keep the lollipop's face, weight,
tracking and measures and its violet, so that the header's mark and the
linear tab's lollipop share one colour and differ by sign; the colour
becomes the token `--color-mark` with the violet's value, so that the
placement can move one without the other. The page also shows the fresh
designers' free candidates in a section of their own (the blue turnstile
with the t's hook kept, the two set in Euler Math: `∴ sequentium` and the
rubricated Fraktur initial), the anchored free candidate, and every dropped
candidate with the reason, and it says what the diligence check found.

Nothing is placed. The author chooses; the placement session follows.

### Round 1: the sets made apart

Both anchored designers read the same brief, `BRIEF.md` in the design
repository (what sequentium is, the lollipop as the member's mark, the
places and sizes, the author's idea first but not alone, the colours, the
fonts and their licences, how a candidate is made, the machine rules), and
drew with the same library, `tools/lib.py` (Figtree outlined glyph by glyph
with HarfBuzz's kerning so that a letter can be left out, moved or
replaced; Euler Math's glyphs; rings, arcs, bars; the serialiser of the six
SVGs and `colours.json`). Neither read the other's directory before handing
in.

**Fable** (`round1/fable/`): *turnstile-t*, the t as ⊢ with the stem to
the ascender, the arm 330 long and "ium" stepped 237 units (which the
supervisor at once noted reads as a gap); *turnstile-fit*, the turnstile
within the t's own advance (183-unit arm, the word not widened);
*turnstile-kern*, the arm 250 long and the letters moved 87 (the drawing
carried into the exchange); *turnstile-mid*, the arm at the stem's middle;
*sequent*, ⊢ before the word; *prompt*, ⊢ after the word; *inference*, the
word under a rule's bar with two premises; and, at the author's request,
the anchored free candidate *free-cut*, ⊣ over ⊢ meant as a stepped S.
Ranking: kern, fit, t, prompt, sequent, mid, inference.

**Opus** (`round1/opus/`): *turnstile*, the t as ⊢ with the t's height and
the arm at Figtree's math axis (−298, the minus sign's line), 260 long,
"ium" moved 98; *rule*, the word under an inference line with nothing
above; *comma*, "sequentium," with a round comma as the square; and its
free candidate *free-assertion*, "⊢ sequentium" with the square "⊢s". It
set every word mark in the lollipop's vertical frame (−768 to 240), so the
two words share an x-height at one rendered height, and every square on the
32-unit grid; it drew the arms with Figtree's own minus sign (109 thick on
the math axis). Ranking: turnstile, free-assertion, comma, rule. It warned
that a turnstile after the word, Fable's *prompt*, is a sequent with an
empty succedent, which asserts the antecedent's inconsistency.

**The fresh designers** (`round1/free-fable/`, `round1/free-opus/`), from
`round1/FREE-BRIEF.md` alone: fresh Fable drew *turnstile* (the t keeps
its hooked foot and height, loses the left arm, the bar alone coloured, in
the nets' blue so that "the house has a hue of its own"), *axiom* (the
word under a line, a new deep green) and *therefore* (∴ before the word
set in Euler Math, the rubric orange), ranking the turnstile first; fresh
Opus drew *turnstile* (the t's height, the arm at the stem's middle, 236

new cinnabar, the word in Euler upright) and *qed* (the word in Figtree
Medium closed by Halmos's ∎, in the nets' blue), ranking the turnstile
first. Both read the fonts' licences in their files before using them.

### Round 2: the exchange

`round2/fable-on-opus.md` and `round2/opus-on-fable.md`, then
`round2/fable-on-free.md` and `round2/opus-on-free.md` once the fresh sets
were in. What the two anchored sets agreed on without having met: the
face, weight and measures; one family colour (the violet), the sign's shape
telling family from member; the turnstile as the t first; "⊢ sequentium"
(Fable's *sequent* is Opus's *free-assertion*); and that the inference line
fails alone (a line needs content on both sides, and at 16 pixels cannot
have it; the square reads as an overline, negation or the linear dual), a
failure the fresh Fable designer met a third time with *axiom*.

Where they disagreed, and how it was settled:

- **The arm's height.** Fable kept the arm in the t's crossbar band at
  the x-height (the word read first, the sign second); Opus put it at
  the math axis (the sign first). Each had tried the other's and dropped
  it. Both rendered the four drawings one above the other at 30 and 80
  pixels (`round2/compare/`, `round2/opus/compare/`); Fable proposed a
  hybrid (stem 656, arm 100 under the x-height), which both drew. Opus
  changed its vote on the lollipop's own logic: linlog's i stays an i and
  the true sign is in the square, so the family's t stays a t; it showed
  that the hybrid does not bridge the readings (its arm leaves the
  x-height line and the eye groups it with the axis drawing) and that
  Fable's 700-unit stem reads as an l with an arm where the t's 656 reads
  as a t. The settled drawing, Opus's *t-literal*: Fable's kerning and
  arm, Opus's stem height and square. Open for the placement: the gap
  after the n, 114 units (the t's own) or 77 (an l's).
- **The assertion's drawing.** Fable drew the 38f turnstile (arm 400 by
  132, as heavy as a stem); Opus drew the arm as Figtree's minus sign
  (109 thick, the weight of the font's horizontals), standing on the
  baseline at the l's height after its round-1 version hung 52 units
  below and looked restless in the band. Fable took Opus's, and its "⊢s"
  square, which tells this candidate's square from the turnstile's.
- **The comma.** Fable: the mark cannot carry its argument (the
  structural rules, which are rules about the comma, are where the
  family's calculi differ), a trailing comma reads as a typo at README's
  head, and the lone comma as an avatar is a closing quotation mark, the
  stock mark of writing tools. Opus answered the last with the "s,"
  square (a comma on the baseline after a letter cannot be a quotation
  mark) and accepted the place: last, the bold alternative, with the
  disagreement stated.
- **The token.** Fable: a token of its own with the violet's value, as
  the brief says, so that the placement can move one without the other;
  Opus first saw no need for a new token and conceded.
- **Dropped by both**, with the reasons kept: *turnstile-t* (a sign in a
  gap), *turnstile-fit* (the 183-unit arm loses the sign at 30 pixels and
  the two per cent of width it saves nobody sees), *turnstile-mid* and
  the hybrid (the sign-first family, shown by *t-axis* instead), *prompt*
  (the empty succedent), *inference*, *rule* and *axiom* (above),
  *free-cut* (it fails as an S and, Opus added, as a cut: two turnstiles
  stacked and facing away read as a step figure or as ⊣⊢, interderivability;
  and the diligence check found Sequentum's favicon to be a squared S of
  bars, one more reason), *qed* (a block cursor before Q.E.D., a
  monogram beside a shape, the Medium weight breaking the family's Bold).
- **The free sets' place.** Fable: none joins the shortlist; the fresh
  turnstiles are shown as evidence (and fresh Fable's blue as the colour
  question), the two Euler Math candidates (*therefore*, *rubric*) as the
  alternative voice, since both fresh designers reached for the formula
  face as the house's own hand, which neither anchored designer did.
  Opus agreed, with two amendments taken into the page's words: fresh
  Fable's hooked turnstile is shown as evidence and as the colour
  question only (a turnstile has no foot, so the hook makes it a t and
  the word's sign differs from the square's), and the two Euler Math
  candidates are said to break the rule of the lollipop's face and to
  read as content beside the tabs' Euler Math sequents: studies in
  another voice, not candidates in waiting. On the arm's height the four
  designers split two and two (the x-height: Fable and fresh Fable; the
  middle: Opus's round 1 and fresh Opus), which is why the alternative
  drawing stands beside the shortlisted one.

### The checks

A diligence check, not a legal opinion; `checks/diligence.md` in the
design repository, with the two researchers' reports beside it.

- **Fonts.** Figtree Bold for every word (the SIL OFL 1.1, read in
  `node_modules/@fontsource-variable/figtree/LICENSE` of the client and
  in the upstream `OFL.txt`; copyright 2022 The Figtree Project Authors;
  no Reserved Font Name). Euler Math for the fresh designers' *therefore*
  and *rubric* (OFL 1.1, read in `.cache/linlog-fonts/OFL.txt` of the

  with the font is not subject to the OFL), 5.7 (no name reserved by
  default; a Reserved Font Name restricts a modified font's name, not

### Decisions

- **One family colour, a token of its own**: every shortlisted candidate's
  sign is the lollipop's violet (#6b46cf light, #958ad5 dark), chosen by
  both anchored designers apart and by one fresh designer: the header's
  mark and the linear tab's lollipop then agree, and the sign's shape
  tells family (⊢) from member (⊸). The token is `--color-mark` with the
  violet's value, beside `--color-logo`, never `var(--color-logo)`, so
  that either can move alone; it joins the colour-vision test's groups as
  the logo's did (38g's guard 2). Fresh Fable's blue (the nets' blue,
  "a hue of the house's own") is on the page as the colour question for
  the author: it wears the nets' meaning (a ⅋ edge, an essential net's
  input) and shows two hues where one grammar binds.
- **The signs in the word's stem weight**, as the lollipop's ring is, not
  a font's hairline, and on the 32-unit grid in the square (one pixel at
  16, two at 32), which is why the turnstile's square strokes are 64
  where the lollipop's are 48 and 40 (Opus's finding: 48 is a pixel and
  a half at 16 and goes soft, as the lollipop's own favicon does).
- **The word in the lollipop's vertical frame** (−768 to 240), so that
  the two words share an x-height at one rendered height; Fable's
  round-1 frame rendered the letters 4 per cent larger and was dropped.
- **The square form is the sign alone** for the turnstile (as the
  lollipop's square is ⊸ laid down; ⊢ cannot be laid down, since turned
  it is ⊤ or ⊥), and the sign with the initial, "⊢s" and "s,", for the
  two whose sign stands beside the word, so that the author can tell the
  squares apart on the page.
- **The exchange's method**: both designers rendered the disputed
  drawings one above the other at the band's 30 pixels (magnified
  threefold) and README's 80 beside the lollipop before arguing, and each
  tried the other's choice in a drawing before rejecting it; the
  reading-order criterion (the word first, the sign second) decided the
  arm, and it is a criterion the placement can reuse.
- **The fresh designers' brief** (`round1/FREE-BRIEF.md`) names what
  sequentium is (the suites the author listed), the places and sizes, the
  constraints and linlog's mark stated neutrally, and forbids reading
  `BRIEF.md`, `README.md`, `checks/` and the other `round1/` directories,
  so that their arrival at the turnstile is evidence.
- **The page** (`tools/page.py`) is one self-contained file with the
  rasters embedded as data URIs, so that the artifact and the local
  fallback are the same page; it is written as the Artifact tool's
  fragment (title, style, body) for publishing and as a whole document
  for the file.
- **GitHub's, crates.io's and the rustdoc's canvas colours** on the page
  are from memory of their default themes, not read in a source (an
  assumption, as 38f's was).
- **The tools**: resvg 0.49.0 and Python with fontTools, uharfbuzz and
  brotli from the nixpkgs linlog's `flake.lock` pins (`tools/build-tools.sh`,
  taken from `linlog-logo`); ImageMagick for the sheets; the system's
  Chromium headless for one screenshot of the page; every run on cores 14
  and 15 in a capped scope.
- **Models**: the partner and the fresh Opus designer on Opus 5.5 at the
  Agent tool's default effort; the fresh Fable designer on Fable 5.1; the
  two researchers on Sonnet 5.5 (web search and fetches); every sub-agent
  named with the prefix `linlog-`.
