# Ordinary logic on ILTP, 2026-11-14

The ILTP library's 274 propositional problems (v1.1.2, `nix build
.#iltp`), decided in classical, intuitionistic and minimal logic by each
method of [ordinary logic](../ordinary-logic.md), and in intuitionistic
logic by deduxit's default beside intuit and intuitR, the SAT-based
provers whose algorithm deduxit's SAT solver follows.

## The methods

The rows are `bench/defaults/sat-default.csv`, written by one release
build of `deduxit-bench` from 18:13 to 18:24: `deduxit-bench run --tptp
bench/iltp/Problems --logics classical,intuitionistic,minimal --engines
embedding,ljt,sat,auto --jobs 1 --copies none --timeout 2 --repeat 3
--repeat-under 1`, in two shards pinned to the performance cores 2 and
3 of an Intel Core Ultra X9 388H, by day, with a single verification
process on cores 7 to 9 beside them. `auto` is the library's default. A run under a
second ran three times, and its time is the median. Each method's time
includes its checks: the SAT solver's derivation, clausal proof or
countermodel, LJT's derivation, and for the default the embedding's
derivation of LK. The embedding's own rows check its derivation outside
the time, so they count a linear proof whose derivation is past the
64 MiB bound as decided, which the default does not.

| logic | the embedding | LJT | the SAT solver | the default | the default's time on the problems all decide |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| classical | 157 | – | 254 | 124 | 346 ms (124 problems) |
| intuitionistic | 188 | 200 | 252 | 252 | 50 ms (186 problems) |
| minimal | 188 | 200 | 253 | 252 | 47 ms (186 problems) |

No verdict differs between the methods or from ILTP's status. On the
186 problems all four decide in intuitionistic logic the SAT solver
takes 26 ms in all, LJT 313 ms and the embedding 11.6 s. The default of
classical logic is the embedding, for its derivations of LK; the SAT
solver decides 254 there with a checked clausal proof.

## Beside intuit and intuitR

The rows are `bench/comparison/2026-11-14-iltp/runs.csv`, with
`ranking.md` and each stream's `env.json` beside them, written from
18:24 to 18:29 by `deduxit-bench run --tools deduxit-default,intuit,intuitr
--tptp bench/iltp/Problems --logics intuitionistic --timeout 10
--memory-limit 4294967296`, in two shards on cores 2 and 3, each run
measured by BenchExec's `runexec` 3.35 as [the
comparison](../benchmarks.md) measures every tool: 10 s and 4 GiB, the
time the whole process's, its start included. deduxit was built from the
commit `zurrvuwo`; intuit and intuitR are at the commits the flake pins,
intuitR with its default, weak clausification.

| tool | solved | proved | not valid | not solved | time on the 254 all solve | median time |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| deduxit's default | 254 | 119 | 135 | 20 | 13.0 s | 3.6 ms |
| intuit | 263 | 128 | 135 | 11 | 2.4 s | 5.1 ms |
| intuitR | 263 | 128 | 135 | 11 | 1.6 s | 5.4 ms |

deduxit's default answers most problems sooner than intuit and
intuitR: it is faster than intuitR on 218 of the 254 problems all three
solve, and its median time is 3.6 ms against intuitR's 5.4 ms. It
solves nine fewer, all of them valid: Cook's pigeonhole formulas SYJ202
at sizes 4 to 9 and the three largest sizes of SYJ206, which intuit and
intuitR solve within 10 s. The larger sizes of SYJ206 also take most of
its time on the problems all three solve, 11.7 s of the 13.0 s. For
every valid problem deduxit also builds and checks a derivation of LJ,
which intuit and intuitR do not make. No answer contradicts another or
ILTP's status.
